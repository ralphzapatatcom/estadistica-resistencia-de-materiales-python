Statistical Analysis: Materials Strength (Aluminum Alloy) 🏗️
This project applies industrial statistics and quality control concepts to evaluate the safety of an aluminum alloy used in construction. The primary focus is problem-solving through Hypothesis Testing and Normal Distributions using Python.

📋 Problem Context
A metallurgical plant produces beams with a required mean strength of 70,000 psi and a standard deviation of 2,500 psi. The objective is to determine:

The probability of failure under a critical safety limit.

Whether a new batch should be rejected using a hypothesis test (Z-test).

The strength value for technical warranties (Percentiles).

🛠️ Technologies Used
Python 3.x

SciPy: For advanced statistical calculations (stats.norm).

Math: For basic arithmetic operations.
